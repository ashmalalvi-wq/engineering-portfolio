# Welding Bend Test Rig (Initial Design)

![Welding bend test rig design](./Weld%20Test.png)

Developed as part of Rutgers Formula Racing's chassis fabrication process in **SolidWorks**, in compliance with **American Welding Society (AWS)** standards.

## Overview
An early-iteration bend-test rig used to validate weld quality **before** committing to full production welds on the chassis. This version was later revised into a different design; this represents the initial working iteration.

## How It Works
- Two thick rods are held in place by a fixed holder, with a test weld joining them at the center.
- A **hydraulic press** applies downward force directly onto the weld joint.
- The rig measures how much the weld **bends before failing**, or whether it **breaks outright** — a direct indicator of weld quality and joint strength.

## Purpose
Used primarily to **test-weld** sample joints ahead of final welding on actual chassis components, catching weak welds before they reach the real build.

## Skills Demonstrated
- Fixture/jig design for standardized mechanical testing
- Applied knowledge of AWS welding code requirements
- Design for manufacturability (DFM) in a test-equipment context
- Failure-mode testing methodology (bend-to-failure analysis)

## Challenges & Solutions
- **Challenge:** The initial thick-rod holder design needed to securely fix the test specimen while still allowing the hydraulic press to apply consistent, repeatable force to the weld joint.
- **Takeaway:** This iteration directly informed a later, revised design — an example of using a first working prototype to surface real limitations before finalizing test equipment.

## Future Improvements
- Instrument the rig with a load cell to quantify exact failure force, not just pass/fail bend behavior
- Standardize specimen holding to reduce setup variability between tests
